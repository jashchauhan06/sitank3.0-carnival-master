About

About SIT

Symbiosis Institute of Technology (SIT), Nagpur is a premier engineering institute established in 2021 under Symbiosis International (Deemed) University, Pune. The institute is committed to delivering industry-oriented education, fostering innovation, and nurturing future-ready engineers.

Est. 2021Symbiosis International (Deemed) University

About SITank 3.0

SITank 3.0 is the flagship startup and innovation platform of the Entrepreneurship Cell, SIT Nagpur. Inspired by real-world venture capital ecosystems, SITank 3.0 empowers students to ideate, validate, build, and pitch startup ideas with strong market potential.
